## Hosted photo processing

The server installs [@netlify/blobs](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@netlify/blobs) (MIT) and [jpeg-js](https://github.com/jpeg-js/jpeg-js) (BSD-3-Clause with upstream component notices). Their distribution includes the respective license notices. The lockfile pins the server dependencies. Submitted photos stay private and are excluded from the source and public site. The submission form asks permission for private review only.

## Method and references

Apekshik Panigrahi’s AnyMachine inspired the reference-first build, scriptable articulation, side-by-side inspection, and export round-trip. AnyBug’s implementation and geometry were written independently. [AnyMachine pipeline](https://github.com/apekshik/anymachine/blob/main/PIPELINE.md) · [AnyMachine MIT license](https://github.com/apekshik/anymachine/blob/main/LICENSE)

The reference photos, research figures, and scan data remain with their original publishers. The repository includes citations only. No external reference images, specimen scans, or family photographs are bundled. See the [ladybug notes](specimens/seven-spot/README.md) and the [mantis notes](specimens/european-mantis/README.md). Each specimen directory includes the part-by-part citations in its anatomy manifest.
