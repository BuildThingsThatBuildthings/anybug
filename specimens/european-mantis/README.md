# European praying mantis

*Mantis religiosa*, a representative green adult female, approximately 55 mm from the front of the head to the end of the abdomen. Open **Specimens → European praying mantis** in the viewer. There are 59 selectable structures, including the folded hindwings under the green forewings.

## Try the grasping legs

Drag either front leg upward or away from the body to extend it. Reverse the drag to close it. The left and right legs keep separate poses. Start at a shin or its label to see the opposing spine rows come apart. The segment and joint names follow the [mantis morphology manual](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5673847/) and a [comparative study including adult female Mantis religiosa](https://doi.org/10.1002/jmor.70013).

**See through** fades the exterior. The brain sits between the eyes, the crop lies behind the long food tube, and a flexor muscle lies within each thickened front thigh. **Break apart** separates the structures, including the normally articulated segments. The anatomy key names every selectable piece. **Reassemble** restores the folded pose.

## Evidence and simplifications

- The shape, green coloration, and model length draw on the [Royal BC Museum species profile](https://learning.royalbcmuseum.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Praying-Mantis.pdf). Look for the dark, pale-centered mark on the inner face of each forecoxa. [Nebraska identification guide](https://entomology.unl.edu/scilit/Maggie%20Heumann%20European%20Mantid.pdf)
- The two controlled poses illustrate extension at the femur–tibia joint and a smaller lift at the base of the femur. A living mantis also positions its thorax and coxa during a strike. The draggable motion illustrates joint relationships. Its timing and angles are illustrative.
- The wing veins, opposed spine rows and abdominal plates are grouped into manageable structures. Each spine row moves with its segment and can also be removed as a unit. The small eye spots depict the pseudopupils from a fixed direction. Tissue colors and organ dimensions are approximate.
- The digestive tract follows a dissection of the related *Hierodula membranacea*. The nervous, respiratory, circulatory and reproductive systems use comparative insect anatomy. The field note for each piece identifies its evidence and links its source. [Comparative digestive anatomy, figure 1](https://doi.org/10.1111/phen.12319) · [All part citations](anatomy.json)
