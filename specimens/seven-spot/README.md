# Seven-spotted ladybug

*Coccinella septempunctata*, adult female. The target body length is approximately 7 mm, measured from head to abdomen. The Blender authoring script uses millimetres and the exported GLB uses metres.

## What to look for

- **Intact animal.** A domed red shell, three lateral spots per wing cover, a seventh black mark divided by the central seam, and ivory markings on the head and pronotum. View the adult photographs beside the model and compare from above and in three-quarter view. The model shows a single representative adult. [UC IPM species reference](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/natural-enemies/sevenspotted-lady-beetle/)
- **Wing mechanism.** Each lifted cover exposes a separate folded flight wing. The mesh changes shape through a sequence of transverse folds. The viewer opens a cover before unfolding its wing, and couples refolding to cover closure. Live animals also use the abdomen and the inner surface of the cover to tuck the wing away. Those contact mechanics and timing are simplified. [Saito et al., high-speed imaging and wing reconstructions](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1620612114)
- **Inside the abdomen.** Follow the midgut, hindgut, and fine looping Malpighian tubules. The model represents six tubules, paired ovaries, oviducts, and clusters of fat body. The arrangement is an illustration informed by dissection. Exact organ volume and position vary. [Bell et al., dissections and micro-CT, figures 1–8](https://doi.org/10.1155/2012/348348) · [Pradhan, alimentary canal](https://www.zin.ru/animalia/coleoptera/ADDPAGES/Andrey_Ukrainsky_Library/References_files/Pradhan39b.pdf)
- **Comparative details.** The breathing trunks, nerve centers, heart tube, and muscle groups show the main paths and relationships. Fine branches, individual fibers, facet counts, and ovariole counts are schematic. The manifest labels comparative evidence. Natural colors are approximate across all internal systems.
